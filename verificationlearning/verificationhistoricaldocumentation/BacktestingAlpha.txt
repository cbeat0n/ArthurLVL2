used as a methodology. Of course dimensions are not always geographical sometimes
a dimension is simply a parameter that constructs a space.

So P = f(a1,a2,a3) where aj is an indicator of momentum, volatility, etc, then 
P = Product(aj) as a sufficient approximation may prove fruitful.
